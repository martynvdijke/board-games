What is stored

  • Identity from the login provider. Sign-in uses OpenID Connect (Authelia on this deployment). The provider is the source of truth for your identity. We receive and store your email address, display name, avatar URL and provider subject, and we issue our own session cookie. We never receive or store your provider password.
  • Account and preferences. Your role, account timestamps, last sign-in, BoardGameGeek username and verification time, sync interval, theme and timezone.
  • Your shelf. The games you own or want, ratings, acquisition date, price paid, condition, location, private notes and imported BoardGameGeek play counts.
  • Your play log. Play dates, locations, duration, quantity, notes, BoardGameGeek play IDs, and the names, scores, winner and first-play markers of the people you record as players. Player names are free text and may identify third parties.
  • Technical data. A database-backed session cookie so you stay signed in, the IP address and user agent recorded with each session, personal API tokens (stored only as hashes, with a visible prefix), and the time a token was last used.
    • {{if .Legal.Analytics}}
  • Analytics. This instance runs a self-hosted Umami instance at {{.Legal.AnalyticsURL}}. It records page views without advertising cookies; no account profile is built from it.
    • {{end}} {{if .Legal.Traces}}
  • Server traces. OpenTelemetry tracing is enabled. Traces describe request timings on the server and are not used to build a profile of you.
    • {{end}}
  • BoardGameGeek. When you connect a BGG username, we query BoardGameGeek's public XML API for that username's collection and plays and copy the result into this instance.

Why it is stored, and the legal basis

  • To run your account and shelf — signing you in, showing your collection, plays and statistics, and keeping your data when you return. Legal basis: performance of a contract with you (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)).
  • To keep the instance secure — rate limiting, session validation, abuse prevention and diagnosing failures. Legal basis: legitimate interest in a working, safe service (Article 6(1)(f)).
  • To understand usage, where analytics is enabled — aggregate page views only. Legal basis: legitimate interest, and you may object at any time using the contact address.
  • To meet legal obligations — for example responding to a lawful request or keeping a minimal record that an account was closed. Legal basis: legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c)).

Who else sees it

Your shelf and play log are visible only to you inside the web app and to clients you authorise with a personal API token. They are not published to other users. The operator and administrators can access the database for operation and support.

Cookies and tracking

The site sets one cookie, {{.Legal.SessionCookie}}, and only when you sign in. It holds an opaque session token, is HttpOnly and SameSite=Lax, and exists so you do not have to sign in on every page. It is deleted when you sign out and the matching server-side session is removed. A separate local-storage key remembers your theme choice; it is not sent to the server.

There are no advertising cookies, no third-party tracking scripts and no cross-site pixels.{{if .Legal.Analytics}} Analytics is self-hosted and cookieless.{{else}} No analytics service is enabled on this instance.{{end}}

How long it is kept

Account, shelf and play data is kept while your account exists. Sessions expire automatically (default 30 days) and expired rows are cleaned up. API tokens remain until revoked or the account is deleted. Request logs and traces rotate on the operator's schedule.

When you delete your account, the account row, settings, shelf entries, plays (including their player-name rows), sessions, API tokens and sync history are deleted immediately in one transaction. Backups taken before the deletion may still contain the old data until they rotate out; they are not used to restore an individual account.

Your rights (GDPR Article 13)

You have the right to be informed (this page), to access a copy of your data, to rectify it, to erase it, to restrict or object to processing, and to data portability. Where processing relies on consent you may withdraw it at any time; where it relies on contract you may stop using the service.

  • Get a copy. Signed in, use Settings or call GET /api/v1/me/export with a token. The JSON contains your profile, preferences, shelf, plays, player names and token metadata (never token hashes).
  • Correct it. Shelf fields, theme and BGG username are editable in Settings. Your name, email and avatar come from the OIDC provider; correct them there and sign in again.
  • Delete it. Use Delete your account. It takes effect immediately, without a waiting period.
  • Object or restrict. Contact the operator using the address above.
  • Complain. You can lodge a complaint with the data protection authority (DPA) in the country where you live, work, or where the alleged infringement occurred.

Requests that cannot be handled by the self-service pages are answered within one month, as the GDPR requires.
